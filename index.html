About

I'm pursuing a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Denver, with minors in Mathematics and Physics. I've found fluid dynamics and heat transfer to be some of my favorite courses in MechE. I enjoy combining what I've learned from these classes with hands-on testing.

My projects range from transient thermal FEA and CFD to a candle-powered thermoelectric generator and an autonomous vehicle running custom C++ control code. Each one reinforced how much I enjoy hands-on testing and iterative engineering.

Outside of class I help build a race car with DU Time Attack Racing, tutor K-8 students in math, and spend as much time as I can in the Rocky Mountains with the DU Alpine Club.
