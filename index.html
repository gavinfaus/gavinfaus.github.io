Background & Philosophy

I am a mechanical engineering undergraduate specializing in thermal-fluid sciences, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), and finite element analysis (FEA). My work focuses on solving critical thermal bottlenecks where standard cooling solutions fail—ranging from multi-hundred-watt AI accelerator die cooling to transient spacecraft thermal control.

Rather than relying on black-box software assumptions, I prioritize first-principles physical validation, boundary layer mesh compliance ($y+$ optimization), and custom Python/MATLAB automation to verify simulation data against analytical theory.

Flagship Simulation Projects

AI Infrastructure

Direct-to-Chip Liquid Cold Plate CHT & Stress Optimization

Designed a high-performance copper parallel micro-channel heat sink configured for a 1000W+ AI processor die. Modeled conjugate heat transfer in ANSYS Fluent to map out fluid velocity profiles, pressure drops ($\Delta P$), and chip surface temperature hot spots, subsequently exporting thermal gradients into ANSYS Mechanical for thermal-stress deformation analysis.

Software: ANSYS Fluent, ANSYS Mechanical, SolidWorks Key Metric: Minimized thermal resistance while keeping pressure drop within pumping constraints.
Civil Space

Spacecraft Avionics Thermal Vacuum (TVAC) Simulation

Developed a transient thermal model for a modular electronics unit (MEU) housing destined for orbital space flight. Applied transient orbital radiation heat fluxes (solar load, planetary albedo, and deep-space IR sink temperatures) to evaluate extreme thermal cycling limits and structural bracket fatigue.

Software: ANSYS Fluent / Thermal, SolidWorks Key Metric: Prevented solder joint fatigue under extreme $-150^\circ\text{C}$ to $+120^\circ\text{C}$ vacuum thermal swings.
Biomedical Microfluidics

Organ-on-a-Chip Precision Thermal Loop

Modeled low-Reynolds-number micro-channel perfusion and integrated micro-heater elements to maintain strict physiological incubation temperatures ($37^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.1^\circ\text{C}$) for cellular cultures, mitigating viscous dissipation gradients across the fluid domain.

Software: MATLAB, ANSYS Fluent (Low-Re Multiphase) Key Metric: Achieved steady thermal uniformity across active culture chambers.
