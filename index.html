Technical Focus

Invisible Physics & Core Competency

Bridging the gap between raw transport phenomena and high-stakes physical hardware. Focused on solving thermal bottlenecks in extreme environments where failure is not an option—from multi-hundred-watt AI accelerator die cooling to spacecraft thermal control systems.

Validation & Methodology

Moving beyond basic CAD drafting. Utilizing rigorous grid independence studies (Richardson extrapolation), boundary layer $y+$ compliance, and Python-based automated numerical validation to ensure simulation data matches physical reality.
